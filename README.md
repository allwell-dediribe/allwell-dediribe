## Project 1: E-Commerce Sales Analytics

Repo: https://github.com/allwell-dediribe/E-Commerce-Sales-Analytics-Project

**The problem:** An e-commerce business had sales data scattered across sources with no clear view of what was actually driving revenue.

**What I did:** I used SQL to pull and structure the data, then built a full model in Excel with Power Query for cleaning, Power Pivot for the data model, and custom DAX measures for the metrics that mattered.

**What I found:** Clear patterns in revenue trends over time, which products and categories actually drove sales, how different customer segments behaved, and operational metrics tied directly to performance.

**Why it matters:** This is the kind of model a business owner can open and immediately know where to focus, not a static report they read once and forget.

## Project 2: Funnel Analysis (Google Merchandise Store)

Repo: https://github.com/allwell-dediribe/google-merchandise-store-funnel-analysis

**The problem:** The business needed to know where in the customer journey people were dropping off, and why conversions were low.

**What I did:** I cleaned the raw event data with Power Query and built the funnel analysis in Excel, stage by stage.

**What I found:** Overall conversion sat at 1.6%. The biggest drop-off wasn't at checkout, it was much earlier: between viewing a page and viewing an actual product. Most visitors left before they ever looked at a single item.

**Why it matters:** This finding points straight at the fix. Instead of guessing, the business now knows exactly which stage of the funnel to redesign first.
